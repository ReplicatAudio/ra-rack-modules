# ra-ulfo — Ultra Low Frequency LFO

Ultra-low-frequency LFO producing sine and cosine outputs (plus their inverses) and a phase/formula output.

## Controls
- **Frequency**: base frequency (0–1 Hz), CV controllable.
- **Range**: ±5 V or 0–10 V output.
- **Attenuation**: scales all outputs.
- **FM attenuation**: amount of FM applied.
- **Phase**: phase shift (0–100%).
- **A / A CV attenuverter**: formula parameter A and its CV amount.
- **B / B CV attenuverter**: formula parameter B and its CV amount.

## Inputs
- **Frequency CV**: linear frequency modulation (10 V = full range).
- **FM**: exponential frequency modulation.
- **Attenuation CV**: overrides the attenuation knob when connected.
- **Phase CV**: modulates phase.
- **A / B** (CV): formula parameters.
- **Reset**: resets the phase.

## Outputs
- **Sine / Cosine / Inverted sine / Inverted cosine**: LFO waveforms.
- **Formula**: computed from the A/B formula parameters.
