# ra-just — Just Intonation Quantizer

Six-channel quantizer that snaps pitch CV to the Ptolemaic just-intonation scale, with root transpose.

## Controls
- **Root**: root transposition in semitones (−1 to 11). At −1 the module is disabled and passes input through unchanged.

## Inputs
- **Input 1–6**: pitch CV to quantize (1 V/oct).

## Outputs
- **Output 1–6**: quantized just-intonation pitch CV.
