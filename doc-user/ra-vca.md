# ra-vca — Dual VCA

Dual voltage-controlled amplifier with per-channel CV and VU metering.

## Controls
- **Gain 1 / Gain 2**: gain; attenuates the CV when patched.
- **Mode**: Linear or exponential (squared) response.
- **Soft clip**: enables tanh soft clipping.
- **Sum**: sums both CV inputs and applies them to both channels, or keeps them independent.

## Inputs
- **CV 1 / CV 2**: control voltage for each channel.
- **Audio In 1 / Audio In 2**: audio signals.

## Outputs
- **Audio Out 1 / Audio Out 2**: amplified signals.
